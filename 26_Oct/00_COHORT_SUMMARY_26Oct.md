Across the 24 learners with assessable work:

- **Dockerfile meets the bar:** 17 of 24
- **Branch protection actually enforced:** 2 of 24
- **SAST that runs and handles B104 correctly:** 10 of 24
- **Secret scanning:** 9 of 24
- **Image scan that actually blocks (HIGH/CRITICAL, exit 1):** 10 of 24
- **Working manifest tag update:** 3 of 24
- **ArgoCD syncing from the repo:** 1 of 24
- **400Mi limit set correctly:** 6 of 24
- **Grafana-managed alerts:** 0 of 24

Recurring defects found by tooling this round:

- **Gates that can't fail:** Trivy `exit-code: 0` (Vijay, Vinod), Trivy lowered to CRITICAL-only with no fix (Abhishek, Nisha, Shreya), Flake8 limited to fatal classes (Vinod, Mamatha, Nashita), `pytest || true` (earlier Nashita).
- **Paths that don't exist:** `bandit -r /app` (Nashita), `pytest tests/` (Nashita), `bash check_vulnerabilities.sh` from the wrong directory (Hardik), Dockerfile in app/ built from root (Nikhil).
- **Manifests that can't apply:** merge-conflict markers (Nashita), broken indentation leaving a null selector (Nisha), a Service selector that matches no pods (Vinod), overlapping Deployment selectors (Nashita).
- **Broken expressions:** `steps.meta` undefined (Amit), `secrets` in a job-level `if` (Tripurakant), a matrix variable outside a matrix job (Ahmed), `$( )` inside an env block (Ahmed).
- **Health checks pointing at the wrong thing:** HEALTHCHECK on `/health` (Vinod), a ServiceMonitor scraping `/healthcheck` JSON as metrics (Vijay), tcpSocket probes (Kiruthik).
- **Over-privilege:** an app ServiceAccount that can create and delete Deployments and Secrets (Vinod), plaintext Secrets in git (Vijay, Utkarsh, Nashita).
- **The 400Mi point missed:** 700Mi (Tripurakant), 1Gi (Shivam), no limits at all (Nisha, Nikhil). At 400Mi the app is OOMKilled about 90 seconds after start (100 MB plus about 3.3 MB/s); at 700Mi it takes about 3 minutes.
